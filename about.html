EXPLORE PLAYER POSITIONS

Every basketball position plays an important role in a team's success. Learn how players contribute to the game through scoring, playmaking, defense, and rebounding.

  • Point Guard: Creates plays and organizes the offense.
  • Shooting Guard: Focuses on shooting and perimeter scoring.
  • Small Forward: Provides versatility in scoring and defense.
  • Power Forward: Contributes through rebounding and inside scoring.
  • Center: Protects the basket and controls the paint.

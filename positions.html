FIVE ROLES. ONE TEAM.

Traditional positions describe common responsibilities on the court. Today's players often combine skills from multiple roles.

POINT GUARD

Organizes the offense, handles the ball, and creates scoring chances for teammates.

SHOOTING GUARD

Creates perimeter shots, attacks open space, and helps defend opposing guards.

SMALL FORWARD

A versatile role that can include shooting, driving, defending, and rebounding.

POWER FORWARD

Often contributes size, rebounding, screens, interior defense, and scoring inside or outside.

CENTER

Often protects the rim, rebounds, screens, and scores near the basket. Many centers also pass and shoot.
